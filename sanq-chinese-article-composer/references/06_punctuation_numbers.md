# 06 Punctuation, numbers and units

## 1. Punctuation

- **Full-width Chinese punctuation** in Chinese sentences: ，。；：？！（）“”《》、. An ASCII comma,
  period, colon or parenthesis next to Chinese characters is a defect (the verifier warns).
  Inside formulas, variable names, Latin-script references and numbers, ASCII is normal.
- **顿号 「、」 only between short coordinated items** (词语并列). Between long phrases or
  predicates, use a comma.
  - 两种作用方向相反、…… → 两种作用方向相反，……
- **Quotation marks:**
  - “ ” (or 「 」 if the institution uses them), never straight ' or ".
  - Nested quotes use ‘ ’.
  - The apostrophe inside a Latin name is typographic (O’Brien), not straight, and is not a
    quotation mark.
- **Titles** of books, journals, treaties and declarations go in 《》. For an article in a
  journal: 《期刊》 for the journal, “ ” or 《》 for the article title, following the chosen style.
- **Dashes:**
  - Chinese uses —— (two em-width dashes) for explanation or a turn of thought.
  - Some supervisors use it often; some projects avoid it and replace it with a comma or colon.
  - Follow the user's or the institution's preference, and be consistent.
- **Spaces between Chinese and Latin script:**
  - A convention, not a universal rule. A common one: a space on each side of a Latin proper
    name next to a Chinese character (按 Smith（2010）), and no space for variable names, which
    act as symbols.
  - Choose one convention for the document and apply it everywhere.
- **No Latin word glued to Chinese characters** (这一效应所associated于……). It means a word from
  another language slipped into the sentence.
- **No foreign scripts:** Cyrillic, Arabic, Hangul, Kana. Greek letters in notation (α, β, μ, ε,
  Σ) are legitimate.

## 2. Digits: fidelity first

- **Every digit is kept.** No rounding, no "余", no "约" that the source does not have.
  - 三百名进出口商, not 三百余名, if the source says "three hundred".
- **Decimal separator is the point:** 0,0415 → 0.0415.
- **No thousands separator:** 3.120 or 3,120 → 3120. Large numbers may be grouped with 万/亿
  (section 4), not with commas.
- **The minus sign is kept** (−0.215 or -0.215, consistently).

## 3. Arabic or Chinese numerals: function, not form

- **Arabic digits for measured data:** 3120个观测值, 0.5个百分点, 15个国家, 低于1, 2015年.
- **Chinese numerals for rhetorical or structural counts:** 三个问题, 六个章节, 五个省份,
  三条渠道, 第一列, 第四章 in prose.
- Chapter titles may follow the heading style (1.绪论 or 第一章 绪论); see `07`.

## 4. Percentages, points and magnitudes

- **A percentage:** digit + %, e.g. 12.5%. **Never 百分之** in academic prose: zero occurrences in
  the reference theses.
- **A difference between two percentages: 个百分点.** "5 percentage points" = 5个百分点, never 5%.
- **A coefficient on a share measured as a fraction (0 to 1):** 0.032 means 3.2个百分点, never
  "0.032 points".
- **Significance level:** 在1%水平上显著 (see `04` for consistency).
- **Large magnitudes in 亿 and 万, not in millions:**
  - 5.2 billion = 52亿 (5,200 millions);
  - 12,345 million = 123.45亿;
  - 2.5 million = 250万.
  - Check every conversion twice: 1亿 = 100 million, 1万 = 10 thousand.
  - In axis labels: 亿美元, not 十亿美元.
- **Pitfalls:**
  - 十余个 = "more than ten". "About a dozen" = 十二个左右; "fewer than a dozen" = 不足十二个.
  - 千分位 is "the thousandths place", not "thousandths": "5 thousandths" = 0.005 (千分之五 only
    if the field writes it so).

## 5. Dates and ranges

- **Years:** 2015年.
- **Ranges:**
  - Choose one form: 2010至2019年, or 2010—2019年 with the Chinese dash.
  - The ASCII hyphen (2010-2019) is common in drafts but inconsistent with the full-width style.
  - Unify, unless it is a supervisor's literal text.
- **"From… to…":** 从2010年到2019年 or 2010年至2019年.
- **Periods:** 处理前（2010至2014年）, 处理后（2015至2019年）, using the same form throughout.

## 6. Tables and figures (numbers)

- Values in tables keep the source's decimals.
- Standard errors in parentheses under the coefficient.
- Asterisks are kept.
- Do not reformat 0.078*** as 0.08***.
